# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Reglet is for engineers, founders, and power users who run multiple local AI coding agents and need their rules, skills, and MCP servers to stay aligned. The first Mac onboarding surface is for users who may not be contributors to the repo and should not need terminal confidence to complete a safe install.

## Product Purpose

Reglet gives every local AI agent one auditable source of truth for global configuration. Success means a user can inspect installed providers, choose exactly what Reglet manages, preview the files involved, create backups, apply changes, and recover without surprise background activity.

## Brand Personality

Calm technical, startup-forward, and trustworthy. The product should feel modern and native to macOS without becoming decorative, salesy, or obscure.

## Anti-references

Avoid Electron-style dashboards, marketing-heavy setup wizards, terminal-only first-run flows, noisy card grids, and any onboarding that hides file writes or makes daemon/sync activation feel automatic.

## Design Principles

- Make safety visible before asking for trust.
- Keep the CLI as the auditable engine and the Mac app as a native guide over it.
- Prefer exact file paths, concrete status, and reversible actions over abstract promises.
- Default every background behavior off until explicitly enabled.
- Use familiar macOS controls and structure so the setup flow feels native, not invented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible flows. Respect macOS accessibility settings including Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast, and Reduce Transparency. Never encode provider or content status by color alone.
